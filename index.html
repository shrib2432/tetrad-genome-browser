Scoring rules and excluded tetrads

    Bright yellow (#FFFF00) marks a low-confidence call from a light band; manual red (#EA9999, #E06666, #FF9999) marks non-Mendelian segregation and removes the entire tetrad. A fill only counts if Excel actually shows it: the workbook paints C–F green/pink by ✓/✗ and whole rows red when YPD is ✗, and in the rad18∆ cross it paints I–J by the Hyg call, so any manual fill underneath those rules is invisible and is ignored here. The pale green/pink and the pale cream (#FFF2CC) are template formatting, not confidence marks. In the rad18∆ cross HphMX is shared by REV1 and RAD18, so Hyg ✗ assigns wild type at both loci and Hyg ✓ requires both confirmation PCRs.
